Twistyard Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Twistyard does not ask for your name, email or account, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data. The only personal data that leaves your device is what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects to show ads, and what Google Play handles when you buy Twistyard Pro.

This policy explains how Twistyard ("the app"), published by Kaselca Apps ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information stored on your device

Twistyard saves your progress in the app's private storage on your phone, and we cannot see it. This includes your best and recent solve times, daily challenge streak, cleared challenges, patterns you make, solver colours, hint count, puzzle unlocks, sound and vibration settings, and your player name. The player name is a random name such as "Cuber-4K2Q" unless you change it; do not use your real name if you prefer not to.

Leaderboards in the app show your own results on this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this permanently.

Camera

The app asks for camera access only when you tap Scan with camera in the Solver. The live picture is read on your device to detect sticker colours. No photo or video is saved, uploaded or shared, and the camera turns off as soon as you close the scanner. You can refuse camera access and still paint the colours by hand, and you can withdraw permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Twistyard Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and handles your payment details under the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your card number or billing details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that Pro was purchased, so it can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall.

Advertising

The free version of Twistyard shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (to unlock a puzzle or get more hints), and an occasional full-screen ad between solves. Twistyard Pro removes the banner and the ads between solves.

To show ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, app version, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to choose and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. If you agree, ads may be personalized to your interests; otherwise you will see non-personalized ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app shows Google's consent message the first time you open it, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones).
  • Avoid ads altogether. Buying Twistyard Pro removes the banner and the ads between solves; rewarded videos are only ever shown when you ask for one.

Children

Twistyard is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you want data removed, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, the EU and UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent.

  • Data on your device: delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Twistyard.
  • Advertising data: change your consent choice by reinstalling the app or clearing its data, reset your advertising ID in phone settings, or contact Google through its privacy policy.
  • Anything else: email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Data retention and security

We do not keep any of your data on our own servers. Data on your device stays until you delete it. Google keeps advertising and purchase data according to its own policies. Data sent between the app and Google is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Twistyard handles data, for example by adding online leaderboards, we will update this policy and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
